How to judge (read once)

For each picture ask only: does this place match the words of the query? Order is shuffled and no scores are shown on purpose.

Relevant: the query's thing is clearly in the square (for change queries: the change is visible between before and after). Not relevant: it is not there. Unsure: you cannot tell at this resolution (10 m pixels; one square = 640 m).

"Near a river" means water within about 1 km. Judge what you see, not what you know about the area.
